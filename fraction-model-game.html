How to play

  • Read the fraction on the gate — the bottom number tells how many equal parts, the top number tells how many to shade.
  • Tap parts to shade them (tap again to un-shade). Then press Check my model!
  • You can't move on until the model is correct — but you can try as many times as you need. 💪
  • Earn ⭐⭐⭐ for solving it on the first try, ⭐⭐ on the second, ⭐ after that.
