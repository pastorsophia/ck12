# 🎮 ClearLessons Arcade — Reading & Math Games

**👉 All games in one place: https://pastorsophia.github.io/ck12-games/**

That page lists every game grouped by grade — click any card to play. Or use the tables below to grab one specific link.

Every game is a single self-contained HTML file that works on computers and tablets, with no login and no setup. Best scores save on the device.

**How every game works:** a correct answer earns a **gold coin with a chime**, a wrong answer plays a **sad arcade sound and instantly restarts** the game, and the coin score sits in the top-right corner. Kids move a themed character or cursor with the **arrow keys or the mouse**, then press **space** (or click) to answer — the answering *is* the action, not just a multiple-choice tap. No timers, no lives to count, no mini-lessons interrupting play. Standards live in these charts only — students never see them in the games.

## The games behind the names

Each game is its own little world with its own character and arcade mechanic, inspired by classic games:

- 🍝 **Meatball Launch** (*Angry Birds*) — pull back the slingshot and land Chef Meatball in the **Persuade / Inform / Entertain** pot.
- 🎭 **Spotlight Stage** — move the stage spotlight onto the literary device each line uses.
- 🔍 **Detective Deduction** — sweep a magnifying glass over the case notes and pin the clue that cracks it.
- 🌾 **Harvest Reader** (*Stardew*) — carry a basket to the crop that answers the passage question.
- 🩺 **Evidence Operation** (*Operation*) — tweeze out the line that PROVES it, what came first, or the best retell.
- 🟡 **Theme Muncher** (*Pac-Man*) — chomp the lesson (theme) of each short story.
- 🎬 **Movie Reel** — frame the right shot with a film clapboard: setting, character, conflict, or plot part.
- 📚 **Owl Library** — a wise owl shelves the right genre, story part, or book term.
- 🎣 **Fish for Words** (*fishing*) — time your cast to hook the synonym, antonym, idiom, or homograph as it swims past.
- 🚀 **Syllable Blaster** (*Galaga*) — strafe your ship and blast the alien with the right syllable count.
- 🌋 **Beat the Lava** — climb with Kiko the gecko as the lava rises; sort syllable types to keep climbing.
- 🌱 **Sprout Defense** (*Plants vs Zombies*) — plant your sprout in the lane with the right prefix, suffix, or spelling.
- 🐸 **Word Hopper** (*Frogger*) — hop across the river to the lily pad with the word that fills the blank.
- 🏃 **Tense Runner** — steer a side-scrolling runner through the correct Past / Present / Future door.
- 🕹️ **Word Claw** (*The Claw*) — move the claw over the prize and drop it to grab the right word part.
- 🌉 **Bridge Bot** — walk a robot across the bridge gate for simple vs. compound sentences.
- ⛓️ **Crane Grab** — lower a crane hook onto the best prediction or reader question.
- 🏎️ **Word Racer** (*kart racing*) — steer between lanes to name the part of speech or fix the sentence.
- 🥔 **Mr. Potato Parts** — grab the right noun or pronoun with a grabber hand to build Mr. Potato.
- 🔦 **Word Detective Beam** — shine a flashlight on the lane with the word's real meaning.
- 🫧 **Rhyme Bubbles** — float over and pop the bubble with the rhyme or poetry term.
- 🔤 **ABC Conveyor** — run a sorter down the conveyor to the word that comes first (or last) in ABC order.
- 🦁 **Sound Safari** — trek the savanna to find rhymes, blends, and syllable counts (with optional read-aloud).
- 🧪 **Phonics Lab** — pour a beaker into the lane with the matching blend, digraph, or vowel sound.
- 👟 **Blend & Dash** — sound out the letters, then dash to the matching word.
- ⛏️ **Root Dig** (*archaeology*) — swing a pickaxe to dig up the meaning of Greek and Latin roots.
- 🔭 **Periscope** — raise a submarine periscope to spot what the clues let you infer.
- 🔥 **Campfire Tales** — hold an ember stick to the anecdote and the point it supports.
- 🧬 **Clone Machine** — power a robot arm to build the correct regular or irregular plural.
- 🔭 **Stargazer** — aim a telescope at the figurative language in each poem line.
- 📣 **Echo Canyon** — shout your echo down the canyon lane with the correctly spelled homophone.
- 🔨 **Stereotype Smasher** — swing a wrecking ball at stereotype, hyperbole, or fair description.
- 🐷 **Punny Farm** — herd a farm buddy to the meaning of an adage or the matching pun.
- 📐 **Blueprint Builder** — hoist a crane hook to name the text structure (cause/effect, compare, sequence…).
- ⚖️ **Courtroom Showdown** — bang the gavel on the claim, the evidence, or the counterclaim.
- 🎙️ **Tone Tuner** — turn a radio dial to the tone or language style of each line.
- 🖨️ **Print Deadline** — stamp the correctly punctuated, capitalized, and spelled copy before it prints.
- 🍕 **Pizza Race** — move the chef to catch the falling pizza showing an equivalent fraction.
- ⚖️ **Balance Buddies** — read the tipping scale and pick which side is heavier (or if it's balanced).

## Offline use

Any game also works with no internet: download its `index.html`, then double-click it. (Online, games load the Poppins font from Google Fonts; offline they fall back to a similar built-in font.)

## Adding more games

Each topic gets its own folder with an `index.html` — one folder = one shareable link. Grade folders (`grade2/`, `grade4/`, `grade5/`) keep same-named topics from colliding; 3rd grade lives at the repo root.
