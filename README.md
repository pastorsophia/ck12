# 🏁 Reading Racers — 3rd Grade Fluency Game

A reading-fluency practice game for 3rd graders, packed into **one HTML file** that works completely offline.

## How to use it

1. Download `fluency-game.html` (in GitHub: open the file → click the **Download raw file** button).
2. Double-click it — it opens in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox), on a computer or tablet.
3. That's it. No internet, no installs, no accounts.

You can also email the file, put it on a USB drive, or drop it into Google Classroom as an attachment.

## The three games

| Game | What kids do | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ **Word Dash** | Read 3rd-grade sight words aloud, one per card, racing a 30/60/90-second timer | Automatic word recognition |
| 🤪 **Silly Sentences** | Read goofy sentences aloud with expression before time runs out | Phrasing & prosody |
| 📖 **Story Sprint** | Read a short story aloud while a stopwatch runs, then see words-per-minute | Passage fluency & rate |

Designed for partner play (one reads, one taps "I read it!"), but works solo too. Best scores are saved on the device, and good rounds earn stars and confetti. 🎉

Word lists are drawn from 3rd-grade Dolch and Fry high-frequency lists; the three stories are original, ~110 words each, written at a 3rd-grade level. For reference, many 3rd graders read around 80–110 words per minute — but smooth, expressive reading matters more than raw speed.
