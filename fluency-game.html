🏁 Reading Racers 🏁

A fluency game for 3rd grade super-readers!

👋 How to play (grown-ups read this part!)

  • Play with a partner: the reader reads each card out loud; the partner taps “I read it!” when it’s read correctly, or Skip to try it later.
  • Playing alone? Read the card out loud, then tap the button yourself. Be honest — racers have good sportsmanship! 🏆
  • Story Sprint times a whole story and shows words-per-minute. Many 3rd graders read about 80–110 words per minute — but the real goal is smooth reading that sounds like talking.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Space = I read it!   S = Skip.
